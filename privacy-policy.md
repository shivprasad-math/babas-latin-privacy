# Privacy Policy for Baba's Latin

_Last updated: October 24, 2026_

Baba's Latin ("the app") is a Latin learning app for students aged 13
and up. This policy explains what information the app collects and how
it is used.

## Information We Collect

Baba's Latin does not require you to create an account, and we do not
collect your name, email address, or any other contact details through
the app.

Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to
manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives purchase and subscription
status information and an anonymous app user identifier so we can
unlock access after you subscribe, restore purchases and see whether
your subscription is active. Offer codes and promo codes are redeemed
through Apple or Google in the same way. We do not sell this
information and do not use it for advertising. Learn more at
https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

## How Your Data Is Stored

Your progress, review schedule, saved word lists, and settings are
stored only on your own device, using standard local app storage. This
information is not sent to us or to any third party.

If you delete the app, this locally stored data is deleted along with
it.

**Progress code.** The app can create a progress code so you can move
your progress to another device. The code is made on your device and
contains your progress. It only goes where you copy or paste it. The
app does not send it anywhere.

**Teacher word lists.** Word lists you type or paste into the app stay
on your device. The app does not use your camera. If you copy a list
with your phone's own text recognition (such as Live Text or Google
Lens), that happens outside the app.

**Pronunciation.** The app reads Latin aloud using your device's
built-in text-to-speech voice, provided by your phone's operating
system. Only the Latin text being read is passed to that voice. No
personal information is sent to it.

## Advertising and Analytics

Baba's Latin does not display advertisements and does not use any
analytics or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

Baba's Latin is intended for users aged 13 and up and is not directed
to children under 13. The app does not ask for personal information and
has no accounts, advertising, or tracking. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children. The only data that leaves the
device is the anonymous subscription information handled by RevenueCat
and the app store, as described above.

Parents or guardians who have questions, or who want information
removed, can contact us at the email address below.

## Changes to This Policy

If Baba's Latin's data practices change in the future (for example, if
accounts, online or classroom features are added), this policy will be
updated to reflect those changes before they take effect.
